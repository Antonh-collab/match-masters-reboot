Ideation best practices / Experience design

A playable is a short story.

We design four connected moments, not four disconnected effects.

01

🪝Hook

Immediately reveal the conflict, prize or irresistible action.

02

👆Interaction

Make the first gesture intuitive. Show instant and legible feedback.

03

📈Progression

Increase the stakes or add a meaningful twist — avoid repetitive taps.

04

🏆Payoff + CTA

Reward completion or build desire for the next level and full game.

Narrative beats, not fixed time windows — pacing depends on the game and channel.
Ideation best practices / Clarity

Understood in one glance.

A playable has seconds to communicate. Every frame must answer: what is it, what do I do, why should I care?

1234
1

🎯One focal point

Contrast, scale and motion lead the eye to the player and the action first.

2

👉Show, don't tell

A hand hint demonstrates the gesture. On-screen copy stays at 2–4 words.

3

🏁Visible goal & stakes

Target, threat or progress is on screen from the very first frame.

4

🧼Clean, safe UI

Minimal HUD, respected safe areas — nothing covers the action or the CTA.

Replayability

Make "one more try" feel natural.

✓ Instant retry after win or fail
✓ Near-miss losses that feel winnable
✓ Score, stars or rank to beat
✓ Each retry slightly different
Rule of thumb: a retry tap is a strong signal.

Gameplay variety

Escalate, don't repeat.

✓ Two or three beats that build up
✓ A twist mid-way: new enemy, item or rule
✓ Meaningful choices: path, weapon, target
✓ Randomised layouts, rewards and spawns
Rule of thumb: no beat should look like the last one.
