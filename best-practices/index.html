Ideation best practices / Creative filter

Five checks before we build.

An efficient brief-to-creative filter: remove ambiguity early, spend iteration time where it changes the experience.

01🤝Player promiseCan the player understand the goal without an explanation?
02🪝HookDoes the opening create curiosity, urgency or immediate agency?
03👆Core actionIs the first meaningful input obvious and rewarding?
04📶ProgressionIs there a clear sense of change, challenge and payoff?
05💰Conversion momentDoes the CTA follow a reason to want the full game?
Ideation best practices / Experience design

A playable is a short story.

We design four connected moments, not four disconnected effects.

01

🪝Hook

Immediately reveal the conflict, prize or irresistible action.

02

👆Interaction

Make the first gesture intuitive. Show instant and legible feedback.

03

📈Progression

Increase the stakes or add a meaningful twist — avoid repetitive taps.

04

🏆Payoff + CTA

Reward completion or build desire for the next level and full game.

Narrative beats, not fixed time windows — pacing depends on the game and channel.

Ideation best practices / Clarity

Understood in one glance.

A playable has seconds to communicate. Every frame must answer: what is it, what do I do, why should I care?

1234
1

🎯One focal point

Contrast, scale and motion lead the eye to the player and the action first.

2

👉Show, don't tell

A hand hint demonstrates the gesture. On-screen copy stays at 2–4 words.

3

🏁Visible goal & stakes

Target, threat or progress is on screen from the very first frame.

4

🧼Clean, safe UI

Minimal HUD, respected safe areas — nothing covers the action or the CTA.

Ideation best practices / Game feel

Game feel makes taps worth repeating.

Clarity tells players what to do. Game feel makes doing it satisfying: every input gets an immediate, readable and physical response.

⚡Instant response

Every input reacts within a frame or two. No dead taps.

🏀Squash & stretch

Anticipation and impact give objects weight and life.

🎆Particles & flashes

Layered VFX confirm hits, pickups and success.

lineareased

🌊Easing & overshoot

Ease-out with a little overshoot. Nothing moves like a robot.

+30

🪙Escalating reward

Counters roll up, combos grow, coins fly into the HUD.

💥Hit-stop & shake

A tiny freeze and shake sell big moments. Use sparingly.

Restraint rule: juice amplifies the core action — it never covers the target, the instruction or the CTA.CLARITY GETS THE FIRST TAP · FEEL EARNS THE NEXT
Ideation best practices / Engagement

Progression makes every action matter.

The player should notice that something changed after each input. We use visual feedback, rising stakes and a payoff to avoid flat gameplay loops.

One interaction

Teach through doing.

Tap, swipe, drag or aim — whatever the core mechanic is, the player learns by producing a clear outcome.

↘
1⚡Instant feedback Movement, reaction, hit, collection or state change
↓
2🔥Meaningful escalation More tension, an obstacle, a combo or a new decision
↓
3🎉Visible payoff Completion, transformation, reward or cliffhanger
One player journey

From curiosity to intent.

Progression bridges the original hook and the final install request.

Design for a satisfying moment, not just a longer session.
Ideation best practices / Conversion

CTA is the payoff,
not a sticker.

Before CTA

🎁Give a reason to continue.

Reward a win, build anticipation, show the next challenge — then make the action obvious.

During CTA

🙌Keep it prominent and honest.

Readable copy, strong contrast, no misleading interactions or unauthorized first-tap redirection.

Best practice: make the install action feel like a natural next step from the gameplay experience.

Export best practices / Technical approach

Export is a design constraint
from day one.

Beautiful gameplay only matters if the playable opens quickly, responds correctly and behaves consistently in the target ad environment.

01

🎯Target

Confirm network, device, orientation and handoff format.

02

💾Budget

Set size and startup-time priorities before asset production.

03

⚡Optimize

Geometry, textures, GLB, audio, effects and render cost.

04

📦Package

Inline assets, minify and apply a platform-specific profile.

05

✅Validate

Test input, MRAID, responsiveness, audio and CTA.

06

🚀Deliver

Export a clean build with tested behavior and traceable version.

Build requirements vary by channel; profiles must be checked against the destination's current specification.
